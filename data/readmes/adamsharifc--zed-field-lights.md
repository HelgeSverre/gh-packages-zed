## Field Lights for [Zed](https://zed.dev)

A dark theme for [Zed](https://zed.dev).

![image](screenshot.png)

Field Lights is a dark Visual Studio Theme inspired by the great Ayu (Mirage) theme but adding some personal taste customizations. These customizations were made by [Sebastián Veggiani](https://github.com/sveggiani).

Ported to Zed by [Adam Sharif](https://www.adamsharif.com).
