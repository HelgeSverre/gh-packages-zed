# One Dark Vibrant

This is a dark theme with powerful and vibrant colors. It's heavily inspired by José Rodríguez' work on a [similar theme for Visual Studio Code](https://github.com/josergz/one-dark-vibrant-theme).

For an optimal visual experience, it's recommended to adjust the `minimum_contrast_for_highlights` and `terminal.minimum_contrast` settings to 1.
