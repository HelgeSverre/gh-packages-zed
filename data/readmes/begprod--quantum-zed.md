## Usage

1. Open Zed.
2. Open the command palette (<kbd>Cmd</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>P</kbd>) and enter _extensions_.
3. Search for the _Quantum_ extension and install.
4. Enter _theme selector: toggle_ in the command palette and select the Quantum in your preferred flavor in the dropdown.
