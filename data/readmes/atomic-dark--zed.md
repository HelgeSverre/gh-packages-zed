# Atomic Dark for Zed

[![License: MIT](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-MIT-yellow.svg)](https://opensource.org/licenses/MIT)
[![Zed Version](https://img.shields.io/badge/Zed-Compatible-blue)](https://zed.dev/)

The darkest take on your favorite themes: Atom, Catppuccin, Material, Afterglow, Rosé Pine, and Vague—re-imagined for
OLED screens with enhanced contrast.

Atomic Dark is an OLED-optimized theme family for the [Zed editor](https://zed.dev/), featuring
multiple syntax flavors designed for deep blacks and vibrant, readable code.

## Features

- **OLED Optimized**: Pure black and deep charcoal backgrounds (`#0f1115`) to save power and look
  stunning on OLED displays.
- **High Contrast**: Rigorously tested to meet **WCAG AA** contrast ratios for maximum readability.
- **Multiple Flavors**: Choose the syntax highlighting that fits your workflow.
- **Polished UI**: Consistent styling across the editor, panels, and terminal.

## Variants

| Theme                      | Syntax Inspiration      | Palette Character                        |
| :------------------------- | :---------------------- | :--------------------------------------- |
| **Atomic Dark**            | Classic One Dark / Atom | Blue, green, and yellow classic palette  |
| **Atomic Dark Catppuccin** | Catppuccin Mocha        | Pastel purple, blue, and teal palette    |
| **Atomic Dark Material**   | Material Theme          | Vibrant purple, cyan, and orange palette |
| **Atomic Dark Afterglow**  | Afterglow (idlefingers) | Warm golden, orange, and green palette   |
| **Atomic Dark Rosé Pine**  | Rosé Pine               | Soothing, pastel syntax palette          |
| **Atomic Dark Vague**      | vague.nvim              | Low-contrast, pastel variant             |

## Installation

1. Open **Zed**.
2. Open the **Extensions** view (`cmd-shift-x` on macOS, `ctrl-shift-x` on Linux/Windows).
3. Search for `Atomic Dark`.
4. Click **Install**.

## Usage

Once installed, you can activate any of the variants:

1. Open the **Theme Selector** (`cmd-k cmd-t` on macOS, `ctrl-k ctrl-t` on Linux/Windows).
2. Search for `Atomic Dark`.
3. Select your preferred flavor.

## Design Specifications

All variants share a common core palette for the UI to ensure a seamless experience:

- **Editor Background**: `#0f1115`
- **Panel Background**: `#12151a`
- **UI Background**: `#15181e`
