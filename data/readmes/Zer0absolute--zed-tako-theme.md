## Development

- Theme generator lives in `./generate.go`.
- Use `make generate` to generate themes when Go is installed.
- Use `make copygen` to copy the generated themes into `~/.config/zed/themes`.
- Generator takes `./assets` palettes and templates to generate `./themes`.
