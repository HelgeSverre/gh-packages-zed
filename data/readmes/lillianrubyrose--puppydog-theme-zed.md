# puppydog-theme-zed

This is my personal purple theme! I have a mild red-green color deficiency and prefer higher contrast themes, so this theme is designed around what I find easy to look at!

I took a **lot** of inspiration from [Catppuccin](https://github.com/catppuccin/), this theme likely wouldn't exist otherwise! I made sure that the colors I chose work well with [their icon theme](https://github.com/catppuccin/zed-icons) as it's what I use personally.

I'm open to changing things for accessibility reasons (especially contrast related!), but if things stray too far away from my personal taste I may suggest that you fork instead. No disrespect, this theme is for me first and foremost.
