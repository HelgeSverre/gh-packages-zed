# Electron Highlighter theme for Zed IDE

To install, go into Zed, open the Theme Picker, then in the bottom corner click "Install Themes". On that page, search for "Electron Highlighter" and install it.

The extension ships two themes: **Electron Highlighter** (dark) and **Electron Highlighter Day** (light) — pick either in Zed's theme selector. The Day theme is generated from the [`palette`](https://github.com/electron-highlighter/palette) repo.
