# Georgy Themes for Zed

Dark-only theme family inspired by simplicity and “accessibility”. Supports all Zed features.

Partially based on my own design system that is made for my own apps (WIP).

Might have issues in some places, like incorrect colors or bad contrast, so, please, feel free to leave feedback.

Also, keep in mind that “Black” variants are not OLED/AMOLED ready, since I didn’t adapt them for that. Maybe will do it later.

## Included Themes
- **Georgy Dark**: Default theme. 
- **Georgy Black**: Deeper, vibrant colors and darker background.
- **Georgy Black Dim**: Dimmer variant of “Black” theme, with colors being less “popped out”.
