  <h1 align="center">Dram</h3>
</div>

Dram is a lush green and blue color scheme for [Zed](https://zed.dev) utilizing the color palette from the science fantasy roguelike epic [Caves of Qud](https://store.steampowered.com/app/333640/Caves_of_Qud/). It is evocative of the venerable [Solarized Dark](https://ethanschoonover.com/solarized/) by Ethan Schoonover.

I have no affiliation with Caves of Qud, or Freehold Games. This color palette is entirely their creation and this project merely plugs in the colors as a free color scheme for Zed
