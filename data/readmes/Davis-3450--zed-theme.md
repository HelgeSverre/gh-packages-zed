# ✨ zed-theme

🎨 Tiny starter pack for building your own Zed editor theme.

## 🚀 Quick Start

- Copy `themes/theme.json` into your Zed themes folder.
- Reload Zed and pick **Dark (placeholder)** or **Light (placeholder)**.

## 🛠️ Customize

- Fill in the color slots in `themes/theme.json`; empty strings/`null` mean “use default”.
- Need guidance? See the [json schema](https://zed.dev/schema/themes/v0.2.0.json)
