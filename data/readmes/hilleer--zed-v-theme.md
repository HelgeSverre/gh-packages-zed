# Zed V Theme

A Zed port of the (great) vscode V theme: Port/adaptation of V Theme (original by @nabinkhair42) for Zed. Original repository: https://github.com/nabinkhair42/vTheme.

For more information about technicalities like the colors chosen, please refer to [the original repository](https://github.com/nabinkhair42/vTheme).

Kudos to the original author, @nabinkhair42, for creating such a beautiful theme!

![v-theme-dark-png](https://github.com/hilleer/zed-v-theme/blob/main/images/v-theme-dark.png)

## Available Themes

Zed V Theme comes in two variants:

* [V Theme Light](https://github.com/hilleer/zed-v-theme/blob/main/images/v-theme-light.png)
* [V Theme Dark](https://github.com/hilleer/zed-v-theme/blob/main/images/v-theme-dark.png)
