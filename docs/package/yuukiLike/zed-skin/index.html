#GreenTree

A high contrast theme collection for Zed. Three dark variants meet the WCAG AAA 7:1 text contrast target in the palette audit, alongside a brighter warm-white Day Vivid.

English · 简体中文 · 日本語

Midnight AAA — pure black, white filenames, bold green keywords and sky-blue functions.

#Four themes

ThemeFeelMinimum audited text contrast
Day VividWarm ivory · vivid green · violet · pink2.73:1
Day AAADeep indigo · vivid colors7.07:1
Night AAADeep black · soft white · regular green7.31:1
Midnight AAAPure black · bright white · bold accents8.29:1

Day Vivid favors brighter green on a light background; that green does not meet AA or AAA. Its other audited text reaches 4.5:1. The AAA label describes checked color pairs, not certification of the whole editor.

#Install

Download green-tree.json and copy it into:

PlatformFolder
macOS / Linux~/.config/zed/themes/
Windows%USERPROFILE%\AppData\Roaming\Zed\themes\

Create the folder if needed. Run theme selector: toggle in Zed and search GreenTree. Restart Zed if the new names do not appear. Official guide.

Marketplace submission is being prepared; install locally for now.
