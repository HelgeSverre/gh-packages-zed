(Old School) RuneScape themed file icon pack for Zed! The theme supports a dozen language and various toolset files/extensions:

Web development: .html , .css (and its variants) .js , .ts , .php , .rb

Python: .py , .pyc , .ipynb

C/C++: .c , .cpp , .h , .hpp , CMakeLists.txt

And more, including: Java, C#, Dart, Elixir, Rust, Lua, bash, SQL, R
