#Atomic Dark for Zed

The darkest take on your favorite themes: Atom, Catppuccin, Material, Afterglow, Rosé Pine, and Vague—re-imagined for OLED screens with enhanced contrast.

Atomic Dark is an OLED-optimized theme family for the Zed editor, featuring multiple syntax flavors designed for deep blacks and vibrant, readable code.

#Features

  • OLED Optimized: Pure black and deep charcoal backgrounds (#0f1115) to save power and look stunning on OLED displays.
  • High Contrast: Rigorously tested to meet WCAG AA contrast ratios for maximum readability.
  • Multiple Flavors: Choose the syntax highlighting that fits your workflow.
  • Polished UI: Consistent styling across the editor, panels, and terminal.

#Installation

  1. Open Zed.
  2. Open the Extensions view (cmd-shift-x on macOS, ctrl-shift-x on Linux/Windows).
  3. Search for Atomic Dark.
  4. Click Install.

#Usage

Once installed, you can activate any of the variants:

  1. Open the Theme Selector (cmd-k cmd-t on macOS, ctrl-k ctrl-t on Linux/Windows).
  2. Search for Atomic Dark.
  3. Select your preferred flavor.

#Design Specifications

All variants share a common core palette for the UI to ensure a seamless experience:

  • Editor Background: #0f1115
  • Panel Background: #12151a
  • UI Background: #15181e
